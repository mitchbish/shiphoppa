How Hoppa works

Five steps. One container. Yours and a few mates'.

Book your share online, send your cargo to our Guangdong warehouse, and pick it up locally on the Sunshine Coast. No CFS warehouses. No consolidator delays. No surprises on landing.

STEP 01

Book your space

Enter your CBM, weight, and supplier details. See the real container cost and your exact share. Pay upfront, your spot is locked.

STEP 02

Supplier delivers

Your supplier ships to our Guangdong receiving warehouse using your booking ref. We photograph, weigh and confirm receipt the day it arrives.

STEP 03

We fill, we ship

Your goods load alongside other vetted importers. The container ships FCL direct to Brisbane — typically 16–22 days at sea.

STEP 04

Cleared on arrival

Our broker partner pre-lodges entries before berthing. The container clears as a single FCL — no CFS holds, no per-line delays.

STEP 05

Local pickup

Container is trucked to our Sunshine Coast yard. Pick up your goods locally — no Brisbane port runs, no CFS bookings.

Common questions

Straight answers, no logistics jargon.

If you've shipped LCL before, most of these will hit a nerve. If you haven't, this is what your freight forwarder isn't telling you.

How is this different from LCL?+

You pay your pro-rata share of the actual FCL container price plus a flat platform fee. There's no consolidator margin, no CFS warehouse fees at either end, and no weight surcharges for dense cargo. The same container you'd be on in LCL costs us the same — we just don't mark it up six different ways.

What does MCL mean?+

Marketplace Container Load — a shipping category we created. The container ships FCL (because at the ocean level every container is a full container) but the space is sold to multiple importers through the marketplace, all paying their fair share.

How do I know my goods are safe?+

Every container is loaded by our own warehouse team in Guangdong. We only accept building, homewares and hardware cargo — no food, no hazardous goods, nothing that could trigger a biosecurity hold and delay everyone. Cargo liability insurance is built in.

What if the container doesn't fill?+

The founder's own stone cargo is the anchor load on every container. If marketplace bookings are light, the container still ships on schedule because his stuff is on it. Your booking is never held hostage to other importers showing up.

How long does shipping take?+

16–22 days at sea Shenzhen or Guangzhou to Brisbane, plus 1–2 days for customs and trucking to our Sunshine Coast yard. So roughly 18–25 days door-to-door, vs 28–40 days for typical LCL.

Do I need my own customs broker?+

No. Our partner broker handles the entry as part of the service — the container clears as a single FCL with one entry covering all importers. If you'd rather use your own broker, you can; we'll provide them with the docs they need.

What are the payment terms?+

Upfront at booking. Once you pay, your CBM is locked into a specific container by date. Bank transfer (AUD) or card. No deposits, no progress payments, no surprise invoices on landing.

Which routes do you cover?+

Right now: Shenzhen and Guangzhou into Brisbane, weekly. Shanghai into Sydney and Melbourne is launching late 2026. Need a different lane? Tell us and we'll let you know if it's on the roadmap.

What's the minimum shipment size?+

1 CBM. There's no hard minimum but the flat $150 platform fee makes more sense at 3+ CBM — under that, the per-CBM math is still better than LCL but the win shrinks.

What happens if my supplier is late?+

Each container has a cut-off date. If your goods miss it, we'll roll your booking onto the next container at no extra fee — usually 5–7 days later. We'll flag it as soon as we see your supplier slipping.
