FAQ

Straight answers.

Will we need to change our workflow?+

No. Forward the emails and documents you already receive. Shiphoppa takes over from there. Later, you can connect Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace for automatic inbox sync.

Do suppliers and partners need accounts?+

No. They keep emailing you as usual, or use secure upload links. Suppliers, couriers, brokers and warehouses can each contribute what's needed without seeing your private data.

What do the three phases cover?+

Order covers everything from purchase order to cargo ready to ship: supplier payments, production, inspections and documents. Ship handles transport from origin to destination port. Deliver handles customs, duty, quarantine, trucking and proof of delivery to your door.

Where do MCL and FCL recovery fit?+

They run on the same automation engine. Smaller importers can find shared container space at better rates, and FCL owners can recover empty CBM when the system proves the cargo is compatible.

What stays under our control?+

Everything runs automatically, but decisions with real risk (payments, customs releases, large commitments) come to your team as approvals with the full context attached.
