FAQ

Straight answers.

Will we need to change our workflow?+

No. Start by forwarding existing shipping emails and documents to Shiphoppa. Later, companies can connect Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace for automatic inbox sync.

Do suppliers, couriers and brokers need accounts?+

No. They can keep emailing, or use secure role-specific upload links. A supplier can upload photos and packing lists, a courier can upload invoices and proof of delivery, and a broker can upload duty notes without seeing private customer data.

Where do MCL and FCL recovery fit?+

They become options powered by the same clean shipment data. Smaller importers can find shared space, while FCL owners can recover empty CBM when the system proves the cargo is compatible and on time.

Can payments really be automated?+

The safe version is one-click approval first. Shiphoppa prepares the payment, attaches the invoice and landed-cost context, flags mismatches, then lets the customer approve. Fully automatic payment rules can come later for trusted suppliers and small recurring charges.

What stays under our control?+

Anything with legal, customs, biosecurity, release or large payment risk requires approval or admin review. The goal is fewer manual steps with clear control where it matters.
