How Shiphoppa works

Five steps. One container.

Book your space, have your supplier deliver to our origin warehouse, and collect from our destination facility.

01

Book your space

Enter your CBM, weight and supplier details. See the real USD cost and your exact share. Pay upfront — spot locked.

02

Supplier delivers

Your supplier delivers to our origin warehouse using your booking ref. We photograph and confirm receipt.

03

We fill & ship

Your goods load alongside other vetted importers. The container ships as FCL — direct, no detours.

FAQ

Straight answers.

How is this different from LCL?+

You pay your pro-rata share of the actual FCL price plus a flat platform fee. No consolidator margin, no CFS fees, no weight surcharges. The same container costs us the same — we just don't mark it up six ways.

What does MCL mean?+

Marketplace Container Load. The container ships FCL — because at the ocean level every container is full — but space is sold to multiple importers through the marketplace, each paying their fair share.

How do I know my goods are safe?+

Every container is loaded by our warehouse team at origin. We vet all co-shippers and restrict cargo categories to non-hazardous commercial goods. Cargo liability insurance is built in.

What if the container doesn't fill?+

The founder's own cargo is the anchor load on every container. If marketplace bookings are light, it still ships on schedule. Your booking is never held hostage to others showing up.

How long does shipping take?+

Transit depends on the route, but because we ship FCL you get FCL speed — typically 10–14 days faster than LCL on the same lane. No CFS delays at either end.

Do I need my own customs broker?+

No. Our partner broker handles all entries. The container clears as a single FCL. You can use your own broker if you prefer — we'll provide the docs.

What are the payment terms?+

Upfront at booking. Once paid, your CBM is locked to a container date. Bank transfer or card. No deposits, no progress payments, no surprise invoices on landing.

Which routes do you cover?+

We're expanding routes regularly. Get in touch with your origin and destination and we'll confirm availability or add you to the waitlist for upcoming lanes.

What's the minimum shipment size?+

1 CBM. No hard minimum, but the flat USD platform fee means the per-CBM savings are strongest at 3+ CBM.
